## Why

Showing an AI what you see should take one key, not five clicks. InstaPaste is a free, lightweight screenshot tool for Windows that turns any key into a screenshot that is instantly copied to your clipboard and saved as a PNG file.

Built for people who work with AI every day: designers, developers, marketers and anyone sharing screens with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot or Cursor. Also great for bug reports, Slack, Discord, Notion, Figma and email.

- **One key capture**: no menus, no editor popping up, no extra clicks
- **Full screen or area**: grab the whole monitor or drag a region
- **Instant clipboard**: just press Ctrl+V wherever you need it
- **Auto save**: every screenshot lands in a folder, named by date and time
- **Multi shot**: take several screenshots within 30 seconds and paste them all at once
- **Multi monitor and high DPI**: sharp captures on any screen setup
- **Tiny and private**: no install, no account, no internet, no tracking

## Get started

1. [Download InstaPaste.zip](https://github.com/J912391923912/InstaPaste/raw/main/InstaPaste.zip) and unzip it somewhere you will keep it.
2. Double click **Start InstaPaste.bat**.
3. Pick your keys, click **Done**. That's it.

## Where is it?

InstaPaste lives near the clock, under **Show hidden icons ( ^ )**.

- **Click** the purple icon to open settings
- **Right click** it for quick capture, open folder, or quit

## Settings

- Choose your own keys
- Choose where screenshots are saved (default: `C:\Users\you\InstaPaste`)
- Turn copy, save and the little popup on or off
- Group screenshots taken within 30 seconds so one Ctrl+V pastes them all
- **Start with Windows** so it is always ready

## Good to know

- Keep the unzipped folder where it is. InstaPaste runs from it.
- Screenshots are saved as PNG with the date and time in the name.
- Everything stays on your computer. No internet, no account, no tracking.

## FAQ

**Is it a Snipping Tool or Lightshot alternative?**
Yes. It is faster for AI work: no editor window, the image goes straight to your clipboard.

**How do I paste several screenshots at once?**
Take them within 30 seconds of each other, then press Ctrl+V once. Works great in ChatGPT and Claude in the browser.

**Does it need admin rights?**
No.

**Why does Windows Security block it?**
If "Controlled folder access" is on, Windows blocks saving into protected folders like Pictures. InstaPaste saves to `C:\Users\you\InstaPaste` by default to avoid this.
