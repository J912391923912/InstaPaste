## Why

Showing an AI what you see should take one key, not five clicks. InstaPaste turns any key into a screenshot that is instantly on your clipboard and saved to a folder.

## Keys

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Print Screen** | Drag to pick an area (Esc or right click to cancel) |
| **F9** | Capture the whole screen your mouse is on |

Don't like these? Click a key box in settings and press any key you want.

## Get started

1. [Download InstaPaste.zip](https://github.com/J912391923912/InstaPaste/raw/main/InstaPaste.zip) and unzip it somewhere you will keep it.
2. Double click **Start InstaPaste.bat**.
3. Pick your keys, click **Done**. That's it.

## Where is it?

InstaPaste lives near the clock, under **Show hidden icons ( ^ )**.

- **Click** the purple icon to open settings
- **Right click** it for quick capture, open folder, or quit

## Settings

- Choose your own keys
- Choose where screenshots are saved (default: `C:\Users\you\InstaPaste`)
- Turn copy, save and the little popup on or off
- **Start with Windows** so it is always ready

## Good to know

- Keep the unzipped folder where it is. InstaPaste runs from it.
- Screenshots are saved as PNG with the date and time in the name.
- Everything stays on your computer. No internet, no account, no tracking.
